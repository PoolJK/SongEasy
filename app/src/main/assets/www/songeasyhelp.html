SongEasy

Easily capture your song ideas and share them with others.

Main elements of the app.

1. Action Bar

Provides the most important actions on top of the screen.

1. Mode selector

Lets you select between the main modes of SongEasy.

View mode

Select to view your song. Zoom and scroll to any position.
Touch screen on left or right side to browse pages back or forward.

Editing mode

Edit your song or create a new one. See Editing Mode for further help.

2. Fit screen

Zoom score to fit the whole screen width.

3. File actions

Find basic file actions here. See File Actions for more.

4. Settings

Apply options for song and app. See Settings for more.

5. Help, Infos, Purchasable Options

Find infos and help here by clicking on the three dots icon, or the associated hardware button on your device.

Here you can pruchase additional available options. At this moment a PDF export.

2. Score

The area where your song is displayed. Zoom and scroll your score and edit your song by working with the elements.

Elements for score can be add and edited by touching or double tap. Most elements can be dragged (horizontal) also.

3. Editing Bar

Placed at the end of the screen it provides tools for selecting, moving, editing and deleting score elements. See Editing Mode for more.

4. Elements Drawer

Opened at the left side of the screen it provides you with all the elements for the score. See Editing Mode for more.
